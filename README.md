# Airbnb Booking Analysis using EDA

# Overview

Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) is a crucial step in understanding and analyzing any dataset. In this project, we perform EDA on an Airbnb booking dataset to uncover insights, patterns, and trends.

The analysis includes data cleaning, visualization, and interpretation of key features such as pricing, room types, geographical distribution, and seasonal review trends.


# What is Airbnb?

Airbnb is a leading online marketplace that connects people looking to rent out their homes with those seeking accommodations.

Founded in 2008

Over 7 million listings across 220+ countries

Offers diverse lodging options — from private rooms and apartments to unique stays like treehouses and castles

Helps travelers find budget-friendly and unique accommodations, while allowing hosts to earn from their unused spaces


# Importance of Analyzing Booking Data

Analyzing Airbnb booking data is important for several reasons:

1) Understanding Market Trends – Identify peak seasons, popular locations, and emerging travel patterns.

2) Enhancing Guest Experience – Learn what guests prefer and tailor listings accordingly.

3) Improving Host Performance – Discover what factors drive occupancy and better reviews.

4) Strategic Decision-Making – Help Airbnb and hosts make data-driven improvements in pricing, marketing, and operations.

5) Enhancing Safety and Compliance – Detect unusual patterns that may indicate fraud or policy violations.


# Key Insights from EDA
1) Pricing Distribution

Most listings fall within a moderate price range.

A few high-priced outliers represent premium listings.

2) Room Type Distribution

Majority are entire homes/apartments and private rooms.

Shared rooms and hotel rooms make up a small fraction.

3) Geographical Distribution

Listings are concentrated in Brooklyn and Manhattan.

Fewer listings in Queens, Bronx, and Staten Island.

4) Price Comparison by Room Type

Entire homes/apartments cost the most.

Private rooms are moderately priced.

Shared rooms are the least expensive.

5) Seasonal Trends in Reviews

Review activity shows clear seasonal patterns.

Certain months receive more guest reviews, indicating travel peaks.


# Steps Involved in the Analysis

Data Collection – Load Airbnb dataset (CSV or API).

Data Cleaning – Handle missing values, duplicates, and outliers.

Data Exploration – Understand variables and relationships.

Data Visualization – Use Python libraries (Matplotlib, Seaborn, Plotly) to visualize insights.

Insights & Conclusion – Derive actionable findings from trends.


# Tools & Libraries Used

Python 🐍

Pandas – Data manipulation

NumPy – Numerical computations

Matplotlib / Seaborn / Plotly – Data visualization

Jupyter Notebook / Google Colab – Interactive analysis


# Conclusion

EDA helped uncover the major patterns and behaviors within the Airbnb dataset.

Most listings are moderately priced.

Popular areas like Manhattan and Brooklyn have the highest concentration.

Entire homes/apartments are costlier than private rooms.

Reviews follow seasonal trends, revealing key travel periods.
